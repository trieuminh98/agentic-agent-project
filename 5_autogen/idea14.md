How about an innovative dish called "Sushi Tacos with a Twist"? 

Imagine the fresh, vibrant flavors of sushi combined with the fun and portability of a taco. Start with a base of seaweed taco shells, lightly baked to achieve a crispy texture. For the filling, we can incorporate a fusion of traditional sushi ingredients with unexpected elements.

**Filling Ideas:**
- **Miso-Glazed Grilled Jackfruit:** This plant-based alternative to pulled pork not only absorbs flavors beautifully but also offers a satisfying texture.
- **Wasabi Avocado Cream:** A smooth, spicy sauce made from ripe avocados blended with wasabi and lime juice, adding a creamy element that enhances the flavor profile.
- **Pickled Radish and Carrot Slaw:** A zesty slaw that adds crunch and a tangy kick, providing a refreshing contrast to the rich filling.
- **Microgreens and Edible Flowers:** For an eye-catching finish and an extra layer of freshness, sprinkle some microgreens and vibrant edible flowers.

**Toppings:**
- **Sesame Seeds and Nori Sprinkles:** A mix of toasted sesame seeds and crumbled nori for that umami crunch.
- **Chili Mango Salsa:** A sweet and spicy salsa that adds a burst of tropical flavor, balancing the savory elements.

**Sustainability Angle:**
To enhance the sustainability aspect, source all ingredients from local farms and focus on plant-based elements wherever possible. The seaweed taco shells can be made from sustainably harvested seaweed, reducing the carbon footprint while promoting ocean health.

To serve, present these colorful sushi tacos on a wooden platter with a side of soy dipping sauce infused with ginger and lime. Not only does this dish celebrate the best of both worlds—Japanese and Mexican cuisine—but it also champions sustainability in a fun and innovative way.
