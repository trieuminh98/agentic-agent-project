**Socially Responsible Shopping Assistant (SRSA)**

**Concept:** An AI-powered mobile application that enhances the retail shopping experience by providing users with information on the social and environmental impact of the brands and products they are considering. 

**Features:**
1. **Brand Transparency:** Users can scan product barcodes or search for brands to receive detailed insights on their sustainability practices, labor conditions, and corporate social responsibility initiatives.
  
2. **Eco-Friendly Alternatives:** The app suggests eco-friendly or ethically sourced alternatives when a user scans a product that has a negative impact on social or environmental issues.

3. **Community Reviews:** Users can share their experiences and rate brands based on their ethical practices, creating a community-driven platform to foster discussions about responsible consumption.

4. **Rewards for Social Impact:** Partner with retailers to create a rewards program where users earn points for purchasing sustainable products or participating in community clean-up events, redeemable for discounts or donations to charities.

5. **Personalized Recommendations:** Utilize AI to learn user preferences in sustainability and offer tailored suggestions based on their shopping habits.

**Impact:** The SRSA not only empowers consumers to make informed choices but also encourages brands to adopt more responsible practices. By fostering a community of like-minded individuals, it drives engagement and promotes social responsibility in the retail sector.

**Next Steps:** Collaborate with sustainability experts and retail partners to develop the app, ensuring practical implementation that aligns with user needs and ethical standards.
